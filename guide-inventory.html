Inventory setup

Set up the stock for every store

Three weeks, thirteen steps. Each step is a task in your Tasks list. Do them in order: each one builds on the one before.

When the stock is set up, the app knows what every store has, where it is kept, who sells it to us, what each menu item uses, and what is missing. Then it can tell us what to order and where stock disappears.

WordWhat it means here
ItemAnything we buy and keep: cheese, cups, gas, soap.
PlaceWhere an item sits inside a store: chiller, freezer, shelf, packaging, cleaning, gas.
StorePizza 99, My Crepe Cafe, Dragon Crepe, MyCrepes Beach Club, Burger Kong, and D'Mall Storage (the storage room).
Pack sizeWhat one buy unit holds: 1 box = 12 bottles, 1 bag = 1,000 g.
ReceiptOne delivery or purchase, typed in the app with a photo of the paper.
RecipeWhat one menu item uses, in grams, ml or pieces. A batch recipe is something the kitchen makes ahead: dough, sauce, batter, patties.

Three rules

  • Same name, same code in every store. Before you add an item, search for it. If another store has it, use exactly that name.
  • Count what you see. Never type a number you did not count, weigh or read. A blank is better than a guess.
  • Stuck? Write it in the task. Open the task, write what you found in Detail, set it to Blocked. Stefan reads it.

The steps

1
Tue 17 Nov

Learn the screens

Open each screen once and look at one item in each store.

Screens
Inventory → Stock, Count, Receive, Transfers · Catalogue → Items, Pack sizes · Buying → Suppliers, Order. Switch the store at the top.
Done
You can open any store's items and one item card.
Who
You alone.
2
From Wed 18 Nov, every day

Record every delivery

Every delivery and every market buy becomes a receipt on the day it arrives. Take a photo of the supplier's paper.

Screen
Inventory → Receive, pick the store first.
Done
Every paper from the store leads is in the app the same day, with photo, supplier and price.
Who
Store leads send you the papers or photos.
3
Wed 18 Nov

Collect the count sheets

The first count sheets went out on 14 and 15 Nov. Get the filled sheets back from every store.

Sheets
Pizza 99 · My Crepe Cafe and Dragon Crepe · Burger Kong and Beach Club. D'Mall Storage has none yet: print the My Crepe Cafe page for it.
Done
One filled sheet per store in your hand, with the date and the counter's role on it.
Who
Store leads.
4
Thu 19 Nov

Map the kitchens

Find out which kitchen makes what, and for which store. Example: does Burger Kong make the patties for the Beach Club? Where is the crepe batter made?

Screen
No screen for this yet. Write the table below into the task's Detail.
Done
For every batch (dough, sauce, batter, patty, cream): who makes it, in which kitchen, for which stores, and how often.
Who
Each store lead and the cook.
BatchMade inForHow often

5
Fri 20 Nov

Check the places

Walk through each store and the storage room. Compare the real chillers, freezers and shelves with the places in the app.

Screen
Inventory → Count shows each store's places in order.
Done
For each store: the places that are missing, wrong or in the wrong order, written in the task. Note any stock kept outside the stores (a freezer somewhere else, the office).
Who
Store leads.
6
Mon 23 Nov

Clean the items

Go through each store's item list with its count sheet next to you.

Screen
Catalogue → Items, tap an item → Edit. + New item for what is missing.
Done
Every item on the sheet is in the app, in the right place and category. Items the store never uses are switched off. Items written on the blank rows are added, with the same name as in other stores.
Who
Store lead when you are not sure what an item is.
7
Tue 24 Nov

Enter the opening counts

Type each returned sheet as the first count of that store. Use the date on the sheet.

Screen
Inventory → Count, pick the store, go place by place.
Done
Every store shows a count on its Stock screen. Blanks on the sheet stay blank.
Who
You. Ask the store lead about unclear numbers.
8
Wed 25 Nov

Pack sizes

Most crepe-store and Burger Kong items have no pack size yet. Without it the app cannot price a gram or a piece.

Screen
Catalogue → Pack sizes: type, Enter moves down, one button saves all.
Done
Every item has a pack size, read from the label or the receipt. Unknown ones: photo the label at the store.
Who
Store leads send label photos.
9
Thu 26 Nov

Suppliers

Every item needs the supplier we usually buy it from, and every supplier needs a phone number and what they sell.

Screen
Buying → Suppliers for the details · item card → Edit for the main supplier.
Done
No item without a supplier. Every supplier has a phone and "what they sell".
Second supplier
The app keeps one supplier per item for now. Where we buy the same item from two places, write the second one and its price in the task.
Who
Store leads, and the receipts.
10
Fri 27 Nov

Pizza 99 recipes

Pizza 99's recipes are in the app, but many are not confirmed yet. Check them with the cook.

Screen
Catalogue → Recipes, filter "Not confirmed". opens after an app update
Done
Every pizza, slice, combo and batch is confirmed or corrected.
Who
Pizza 99 cook.
11
Tue 1 Dec

Burger Kong recipes

Stefan gave the Burger Kong cooks a recipe list to weigh. Get it back and type it in: the batches first, then every menu item.

Screen
Catalogue → Recipes → + New recipe, name exactly as on the till. opens after an app update
Done
Every Burger Kong menu item and batch has its ingredients and is marked confirmed. Lines nobody weighed stay out.
Who
Burger Kong cook, store lead.
12
Thu 3 Dec

Crepe recipes

One recipe list covers My Crepe Cafe, Dragon Crepe and the Beach Club. Same steps as Burger Kong.

Screen
Catalogue → Recipes, for each of the three stores. opens after an app update
Done
Every crepe, shake, coffee and batch has its ingredients in all three stores. Dragon Crepe's two dragonfruit shakes too.
Who
Crepe cooks, store leads.
13
Fri 4 Dec

Where stock really sits

Second count in every store, then look at the gaps. List every place we keep stock, including the storage room and anything kept for two stores. The storage room moves into the Dragon Crepe building when the office moves.

Screen
Inventory → Count, then Stock to see the gaps.
Done
A list in the task: every stock place (store, storage room, other), what is kept there, and the ten biggest gaps between the count and the app.
Who
Store leads.

Paper only where it helps

Count sheets and recipe weighing lists go into the kitchen on paper. Everything else you do on the iPad.
